1. Journey Details

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Refund is calculated according to the time remaining before bus departure:

  • More than 24 hours before departure: 90% refund
  • 12 hours to 24 hours before departure: 75% refund
  • 4 hours to less than 12 hours before departure: 50% refund
  • 4 hours or less before departure: 0% refund

Cancelled bookings remain visible in booking history. Cancelled seats become available again. Refund shown is a demo calculation; no actual payment is processed.
